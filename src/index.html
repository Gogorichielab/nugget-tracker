HOW THE
BREAKFAST HAPPENED

When a Cubs pitcher recorded three strikeouts in a single inning during a qualifying home game at Wrigley Field, fans could claim a free Chicken Biscuit or Spicy Chicken Biscuit at participating Chick-fil-A locations during the next day's breakfast hours, subject to the offer's terms.
