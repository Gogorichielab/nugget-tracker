⚾ TL;DR

This site tracks Cubs pitcher strikeout events so you know when to get free Chick-fil-A nuggets. We do not collect your personal information, we do not sell your data, and we do not run advertising. Cloudflare provides basic, privacy-friendly analytics so we know the site is working.

📋 What Information We Collect

This site collects no personal information from public visitors. Specifically:

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any contact information.
  • We do not use cookies on the public-facing site.
  • We do not use advertising trackers, pixels, or fingerprinting.

The only data stored in our database consists of Cubs pitcher event records (game dates, pitcher names, and inning numbers) sourced from the MLB Stats API. This is public sports data, not personal information.

📊 Analytics (Cloudflare)

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how many people visit the site and whether pages load successfully.

  • It does not use cookies.
  • It does not track individual users across sessions or sites.
  • It does not collect personally identifiable information.
  • It provides aggregated metrics (for example page views and country-level traffic).

For more information, see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

⚾ MLB Data

Game data is sourced from the MLB Stats API. Pitcher names displayed on this site are the names of public professional athletes.

🔗 Third-Party Services

This site uses the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare — CDN and privacy-friendly analytics. Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft Azure — Hosting and data storage. Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts — Bebas Neue and DM Sans from Google's CDN. Privacy Policy
  • MLB Stats API — Public baseball statistics data.

👦 Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
